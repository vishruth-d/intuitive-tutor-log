Tutor Log terms

Tutor Log is a tool for recording tutoring sessions and preparing your invoice spreadsheet.

You own and control your spreadsheet. Enter only information you are authorised to use, check attendance and invoice totals before sharing them, and manage your file’s sharing permissions.

Google and Cloudflare availability and service limits apply. Keep a backup of records you need. Contact the support address on the Google consent screen if you need help.
