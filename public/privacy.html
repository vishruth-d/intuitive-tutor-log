Tutor Log privacy

Tutor Log creates an invoice spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. Your name, hourly rate, student roster, attendance and session times go directly from your browser to Google.

The app asks for your email address to identify the signed-in account, and access to specific Drive files created or opened with this app. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Your configuration is stored in spreadsheet metadata and recovered when you sign in on another device. Anyone you give access to the spreadsheet may be able to read its contents and metadata. Keep its sharing settings appropriate for student information.

Google access tokens stay in browser memory and are cleared when you sign out or close the page. Temporary retry identifiers and hashes may be kept in session storage until a save is confirmed or the tab is closed. Student data is not stored in browser storage.

Cloudflare serves the website and processes ordinary web requests, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. Invoice data and Google access tokens are not sent to Cloudflare. Google also provides sign-in and fonts. The app has no advertising, analytics or separate tutor database.

You control retention: delete the invoice from Drive to remove its data, and remove Tutor Log from your Google account’s third-party connections to revoke access. Signing out of this website does not revoke the Google grant.

The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training. Use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

For support or deletion questions, contact the support address shown on the app’s Google consent screen.
